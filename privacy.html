Privacy Policy — Clip Factory

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Who uses this app

Clip Factory is a private tool used only by the owner of the Frontier Tech Talks YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to update or delete videos that the app uploaded to that channel.

It does not read anyone else's channel, comments or personal data, and it does not access Gmail, Drive or contacts.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Video files and their titles stay on that computer until they are uploaded to YouTube.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to upload and manage the owner's videos.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube's own terms apply to uploaded videos: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
