Frontier Tech Talks

Frontier Tech Talks is a YouTube channel that publishes short vertical clips from long-form talks and interviews about technology. Each clip is cut from a public source and adds a written hook and captions.

The channel's owner uses a small private tool, "Clip Factory", to upload these clips to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to anyone else.
